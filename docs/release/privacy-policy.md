# Privacy Policy — Family Tree

_Last updated: 2026-10-02_

Family Tree does not collect, transmit, or store your data on any server we control. We operate no server, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

## What the app stores
Your trees and the people in them — names, sex, birth and death dates and places, origin, notes, photos you attach, and how people are related — plus your settings and a change history. These often describe your relatives, so treat them with the same care as your own data. Everything is kept in a database and a photo folder on your device.

On the device the app also keeps one daily copy of your trees for the last 7 days, so you can restore an earlier state.

## Photos
When you add a photo to a person, the system photo picker opens and only the photo you choose is copied into the app, resized. The app does not read the rest of your library.

## What leaves your device
- **iCloud Drive backup** — on by default when iCloud Drive is enabled on your phone; Settings → Daily iCloud Drive backup shows whether it is on and when it last ran, and turns it off. While on, once a day after a change (and right after you delete a tree, unless none are left: an empty phone never replaces a backup), a backup file (trees, photos, change history) replaces the previous one in your own iCloud Drive (Files → iCloud Drive → Family Tree), in your Apple account and your storage quota. We have no access to it. Turning it off stops new copies; the last one stays until you delete it.
- **Export** — a `.zip` file you create and send wherever you choose via the share sheet.
- **Import** — files you pick (this app's backups or GEDCOM) are read on the device.

On a fresh install with no trees of your own, the app restores your trees from that iCloud Drive backup if one exists.

Nothing else leaves the device.

## Analytics and advertising
None. No analytics SDK, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites. No account or sign-in.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Deleting your data
- **Delete a person or a tree** — removes them and their entries in the change history. So that a deletion can be undone, the on-phone daily copies of the last 7 days (Settings → Restore a daily copy) still hold it, with its photos, until they age out.
- **Settings → Remove all app data** — deletes everything the app keeps on the phone at once: trees, photos, change history, settings, daily copies and the demo data. If an iCloud Drive backup exists, you choose whether to delete it too or keep it (the daily iCloud backup is then turned off so the kept copy isn't overwritten).
- Deleting the app also removes everything on the phone.
- Files you exported and shared, or a kept iCloud Drive backup, are yours to delete in the Files app or wherever you sent them.
